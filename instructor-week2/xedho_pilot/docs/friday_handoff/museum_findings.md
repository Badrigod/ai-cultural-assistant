Existing inventory information:

Additional information required:

- Traditional placement inside the Somali aqal.
- Meaning of geometric patterns.

Museum response: Pending documentation.

### KHH-004 — Jalxaad

Existing inventory information:
- Fired clay or terracotta vessel.
- Narrow neck and raised handles.
- Used primarily for water storage and cooling.

Additional information required:
- Correct museum classification.
- Traditional manufacturing method.
- Historical use and origin.
